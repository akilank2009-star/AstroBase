There is No Single Path

Maybe you've already got an idea of where you want to go. Maybe you're fascinated by black holes, dreaming about becoming an astronomer, curious about spacecraft, or wondering what it would actually take to work in space science.

You don't have to have everything figured out yet. There are competitions to discover, research programmes to find, books and courses to learn from, universities to explore, and countless fields and pathways that you may not even know exist yet.

There is no single path into space science. Explore what interests you, follow what makes you curious, and take your next step.
