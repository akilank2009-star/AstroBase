ABOUT ASTROBASE

A place to find your path into space science.

Astrobase is a platform designed to bring together opportunities, knowledge, universities and guidance for students exploring astronomy, astrophysics and the wider space sciences.

THE IDEA

What is Astrobase?

Finding a path into space science can involve searching across universities, research programmes, competitions, internships, courses and countless sources of information.

Astrobase brings these pieces together into one organised ecosystem, helping students discover what exists, understand their options and build their own journey.

THE ECOSYSTEM

Everything begins with discovery.

Opportunities

Discover competitions, internships, research programmes, scholarships, workshops and other ways to gain experience.

Resources

Find books, courses, papers, websites, videos, tools and other resources to support learning.

Universities

Explore universities and programmes relevant to astronomy, astrophysics, physics and space science.

Guidance

Build a personal profile and eventually use Astrobase's intelligent tools to receive more relevant guidance and recommendations.

FOR STUDENTS

Your journey is personal.

Astrobase is built around the idea that there is no single route into space science.

Students can explore opportunities, discover resources, research universities and gradually build a profile around their own interests and goals.

FOR ORGANISATIONS

Connect knowledge with the people looking for it.

Organisations can contribute opportunities, resources and university information to the Astrobase ecosystem.

Contributions are reviewed before publication, helping keep the platform organised and reliable.

THE VISION

From curiosity to exploration.

Astrobase aims to become a growing ecosystem for students and organisations connected to space science, making discovery more accessible and helping people turn curiosity into meaningful action.
